Bildungsbrücke International was created to support families who need clearer guidance across different education systems and languages.

教育桥国际旨在帮助需要跨教育体系、跨语言理解信息的家庭做出更清晰的升学选择。

The service is built on direct international school experience, familiarity with the IB system, and first-hand knowledge of Canadian university applications.
